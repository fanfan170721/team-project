# Team Contract

The rules below record our team's agreed working arrangements.

---
## Purpose of this Contract

This contract sets out shared expectations and commitments for how our team will collaborate during the course. It is designed to promote accountability, professionalism, and mutual support as we complete individual preparation, readiness assurance tests, in-class activities, and our course project.

---
## Team Norms and Expectations

### Communication

* We use our existing team group chat to communicate about the project outside class.
* Each member normally replies to team messages within 24 hours.
* If a member expects to miss a task deadline, they notify the team in advance.
* We listen to each other's views and give every member a chance to participate in discussions.

---

### Weekly Progress Updates

* We meet once a week to discuss progress and any difficulties with our tasks.
* Regular meeting day and time: **Every Sunday at 5:00 p.m., Toronto local time (America/Toronto).**

### Contributions and Code Review

* Each member is responsible for their own assigned tasks.
* Each member submits their own work using their own GitHub account.
* We review each other's code.

---

## Decision Making

We discuss project decisions together and work toward agreement. If a disagreement remains unresolved, we follow the conflict resolution process below.

---
## Conflict resolution

We first discuss the disagreement and listen to each member's perspective. If we cannot resolve it together, we consult a TA as a team.

---

## Accountability

Each member is responsible for communicating their progress, completing their assigned work, and participating in code review. Members who encounter difficulties raise them with the team and give advance notice if a deadline is at risk.

---

By signing below, we acknowledge that we have read, discussed, and agreed to these terms. We understand that this contract is binding for the duration of the course and may be revised with the agreement of all team members.
